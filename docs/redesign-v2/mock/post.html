1Setup

Let X be the n × d design matrix and y the vector of labels, each 0 or 1. The model predicts a probability p = σ(Xw) for every row, where σ(z) = 1 / (1 + e−z) is the logistic function and w holds the weights to learn.

The loss is the mean negative log-likelihood of the labels under those probabilities.

2The gradient

Differentiating the loss with respect to w gives a short expression, because the derivative of σ cancels against the logarithm:

∇L(w) = 1⁄n X⊤(σ(Xw) − y)(1)
